Derived code
------------
Parts of OCTAVE's chart preview renderer (src/renderer/src/components/
chartPreviewModules/, in particular constants.ts, Highway.tsx, Strikeline.tsx
and BeatGrid.tsx) reproduce layout values, colour values and visual behaviour
from YARG's C# sources (TrackPlayer.cs, FretArray.cs, GenericTrackPlayer.cs),
Track.shadergraph and RectangularFret.prefab so that the preview matches the
game. Those sources are also LGPL-3.0-or-later, Copyright (c) YARC and the
YARG contributors.
